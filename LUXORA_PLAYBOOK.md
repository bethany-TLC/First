# The Luxora Collective — Playbook

## What Luxora does

Growth partner for independent med spas ($500K–$2M/year revenue). Done-with-you model:

1. **Revenue Leak Audit** — entry point, diagnoses where the practice is losing revenue
2. **Market Blueprint** — positioning and offer strategy
3. **Growth Engine installation** — memberships, package architecture, staff sales training, AI follow-up, CRM, partnerships, reputation engine, LTV frameworks
