Revenue Leaks to Probe on This Call
Ask how they are using Zenoti beyond booking. Zenoti has built-in marketing automation, recall messaging, and retention tools. If they are not using those features, they are leaving automated revenue on the table every single month
Ask about their membership program. How many members do they currently have, and what is the renewal rate? If the membership exists but is not being actively sold and promoted, it is generating a fraction of what it could
Ask about retail attachment. They carry four premium skincare lines (Revision, Epionce, Skinbetter, Colorescience) - the highest-margin revenue in any med spa. If providers are not consistently recommending and selling retail at every visit, they are walking past money constantly
Ask about their semaglutide and weight loss program. This is one of the most in-demand services in the country right now. If they are not actively marketing it - paid ads, social content, referral partnerships with primary care - they are almost certainly under-enrolled
Ask about their HRT and NAD+ programs. These are high-ticket, high-retention services. Patients on bioidentical HRT and NAD+ injections tend to be long-term clients with high lifetime value. If there is no dedicated funnel for these services, it is a significant missed opportunity
Ask about gift card sales and how they promote them. Gift cards are a low-effort, high-return revenue stream that most clinics barely market
Systems and Staff to Probe on This Call
Ask how new providers (Olivia, Urska) are onboarded into Jeannine's clinical and service standards. With multiple injectors, brand consistency and quality control become critical. If every provider delivers a slightly different experience, client retention suffers
Ask how they handle rebooking at checkout. A multi-service clinic with injectables, wellness protocols, and skin treatments should have patients returning on a cadence - but that only happens if there is a system prompting it at every visit
Ask how they collect and follow up on patient feedback. With 5-star reviews across platforms, they clearly have happy clients - but are those clients being activated as referral sources? A structured referral program could double new client volume without any ad spend
Ask about their no-show and cancellation rate and how it is managed. Injectable and laser appointment slots are high-value. An unmanaged no-show problem is a direct revenue drain that a simple system can fix
Ask how treatment plans are structured. Are patients being educated at every visit about the full range of services and given a personalized long-term treatment roadmap? Or are they booking one service at a time? The difference in average client value between these two approaches is dramatic
